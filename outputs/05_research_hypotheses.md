## 1. Primary Research Question

> **Can the predictive directional signal generated by the frozen V22-R ensemble be transformed into a positive net expectancy trading system after realistic transaction costs (12 basis points standard taker friction) without retraining or cherry-picking?**

## 3. Strict Scientific Controls & Anti-Overfitting Rules

1. **Frozen Baseline:** V22-R model weights, normalization vectors, and feature definitions remain 100% frozen. Zero gradient steps or parameter retuning.
2. **Sealed Window Integrity:** V23 (`2025-07-05` to `2025-07-11` Istanbul) remains sealed. No candle from this window is queried.
3. **Control Status:** The Fresh Test window (`2025-07-26` to `2025-08-01` Istanbul) is a **known control**, not an untouched validation set. Performance on Fresh Test is recorded to detect over-fitting and regime shifts, not to declare out-of-sample success.
4. **All Losses Preserved:** No strategy candidate is dropped or edited post-hoc. All failed hypotheses are documented and reported in the strategy registry.
