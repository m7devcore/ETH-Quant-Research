## 9. Frozen Official V13 Rule

The following was frozen **before any V13 Hidden data were queried**.

### A. 4H trend state
Using the latest completed native Binance 4H candle:

**UPTREND**
- `ER(6) ≥ 0.50`
- `EMA5 > EMA13`
- EMA13 slope over the previous 3 completed 4H bars is positive.

**DOWNTREND**
- `ER(6) ≥ 0.50`
- `EMA5 < EMA13`
- EMA13 slope over the previous 3 completed 4H bars is negative.

Otherwise: no signal.

### B. 15m pullback
Using completed 15m candles:
- calculate close-to-close displacement over the last **3 completed 15m candles**;
- it must point **opposite** the 4H trend;
- magnitude must be **≥ 0.60 × 15m ATR(7)**.

### C. 5m resumption confirmation
The latest **two completed 5m candle bodies** must both point **with the 4H trend**.

### D. Prediction
Predict the **next 15m candle** in the 4H trend direction.

### E. Expiry / exclusions
- one 15m candle only;
- no cooldown;
- no D1 filter;
- no volatility filter;
- no failed-breakout branch;
- all other states = **NO TRADE**.

## 21. Scientific Interpretation

V13 exposed a new distinction:

> **Strong trend ≠ every shallow pullback is a continuation opportunity.**

The rule correctly identified a powerful bearish 4H regime, but during the Hidden crash it frequently entered **after only a shallow 15m counter-move**. Two bearish 5m bodies were often just local noise inside a larger rebound, not genuine re-acceleration.

This explains why:
- every Hidden signal was DOWN;
- the market still produced seven next-candle UP closes;
- several misses had extremely large adverse excursions;
- stronger 5m confirmation thresholds did not fix the problem;
- the successful signals, in hindsight, had much deeper pullbacks.

V13 therefore does **not** invalidate regime-first analysis. It shows that regime and entry timing need separate state variables. A trend can be correctly classified while the immediate continuation entry is wrong.
