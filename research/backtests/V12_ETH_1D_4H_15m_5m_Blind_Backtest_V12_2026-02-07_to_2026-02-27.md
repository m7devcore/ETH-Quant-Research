## 3. What V1–V11 Contributed

The prior files were treated as a learning history, not as permission to reuse a previous rule blindly.

- **V1:** Trigger → Confirmation → Invalidation → Flip → Reassessment. Accepted price action can invalidate a lagging thesis.
- **V2:** Regime before direction. `NO TRADE` is a legitimate outcome; forced prediction destroys calibration.
- **V3:** Regime expiry/reset matters. 5m is better as confirmation than as primary direction. Failed-breakout fading showed some promise, but on small samples.
- **V4:** Early transition is dangerous. One lower-timeframe reversal candle is not enough by itself.
- **V5:** Failure of acceptance is more meaningful than a visually attractive wick alone. Repeated attacks and persistence matter.
- **V6/V7:** 4H-trend pullback continuation can work in the right regime but is not universal.
- **V8:** Low efficiency ratio does not automatically mean a stable range.
- **V9:** Local value/midpoint migration matters. Prefer parameter neighborhoods over magic thresholds.
- **V10:** Strong displacement does not automatically equal exhaustion; overlapping exhaustion conditions may actually describe expansion.
- **V11:** Large 90m displacement + low 15m ER + one 5m counter candle looked excellent in Study but collapsed OOS. A local pause can sit inside continuing price discovery.

V12 therefore asked a narrower question: **Can an objectively failed local breakout be faded only when the surrounding local value is sufficiently stable?**

## 7. V12 Candidate Families

### 7.1 Delayed / repeated exhaustion
Waiting one extra candle did not solve V11's problem. A representative delayed-exhaustion candidate produced **25/42 = 59.52%**. A repeated-trigger candidate reached **11/16 = 68.75%**, but the sample was small and the early segment was only **50%**. It was not selected.

### 7.2 Accepted-breakout continuation
Continuation after apparent acceptance was explicitly tested because V11 taught that many “exhaustion” events were actually ongoing repricing. However, the direct continuation family performed poorly:

## 8. Failed-Breakout Family Discovery

The most coherent Study family was a **failed local breakout → one-candle fade**:

1. Define a prior 15m range.
2. Require price to trade materially beyond the range edge.
3. Require the signal candle to close materially back inside that old range.
4. Reject cases where the local range midpoint/value is migrating too quickly.
5. Fade the failed breakout for exactly the next 15m candle.

This directly uses the V5 principle “failure of acceptance > visual wick” while adding V9's value-migration control.

## 9. Anti-Overfit Selection

Several higher in-sample scores were intentionally rejected:
- A range-width constrained version reached **12/15 = 80%**, but only 15 signals.
- Some tighter midpoint variants reached roughly **72%**, but had weak middle-segment performance around **40–50%**.
- Stronger 5m confirmation reduced sample size without producing better temporal stability.
- Mandatory 4H filters did not improve the pre-reveal Study family enough to justify extra complexity.

The selected rule was not the highest Study score. It was selected because it had a larger sample and comparatively stable chronological performance.

## 10. Frozen Official V12 Rule

The following rule was frozen **before any Hidden query**:

### Reference structure
- `Range32`: high/low of the **32 completed 15m candles before the signal candle**.
- `ATR7`: 15m ATR(7).
- Compute the midpoint of `Range32`.
- Compute the same 32-bar midpoint ending **6 candles earlier**.
- `midpoint_drift = |mid_now - mid_shifted6| / ATR7`.

### Upper failed breakout → DOWN
Signal only if:
- signal-candle high ≥ prior range high + **0.10 × ATR7**
- signal-candle close ≤ prior range high − **0.05 × ATR7**
- midpoint drift ≤ **1.00 × ATR7**

Then predict the **next 15m candle DOWN**.

### Lower failed breakout → UP
Signal only if:
- signal-candle low ≤ prior range low − **0.10 × ATR7**
- signal-candle close ≥ prior range low + **0.05 × ATR7**
- midpoint drift ≤ **1.00 × ATR7**

Then predict the **next 15m candle UP**.

### Expiry / exclusions
- Prediction expires after **one 15m candle**.
- No mandatory 5m filter.
- No mandatory 4H filter.
- No RSI, volume, cooldown, or extra exhaustion branch.
- All other situations = **NO TRADE**.
- A flat next-candle close is scored as MISS because the directional prediction did not occur.

The key distinction now is:

> **Local rejection is not the same as higher-timeframe rejection.**

A local wick plus close-back becomes much less trustworthy when the breakout direction is aligned with an already accepted 4H directional state. This is especially visible on:
- **21 Feb 17:30** — upside break aligned with 4H uptrend.
