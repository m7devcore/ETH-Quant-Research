Borderline higher-timeframe trend strength is not sufficient.

V8 must now test expansion without discarding those lessons.

**close position ≤30% of local range**

## Layer 4 — RSI Stretch

Bullish previous candle:
**RSI(7) ≥55**

Bearish previous candle:
**RSI(7) ≤45**

---

## Layer 5 — Expansion Filter

Relative volume:

**previous 15m volume / average previous 20 volumes ≤2.5**

If volume is above 2.5:

# NO FADE

This is designed to reduce fading genuine expansion.

---

## Direction

If all conditions pass:

- strong bullish edge candle → predict next 15m **DOWN**
- strong bearish edge candle → predict next 15m **UP**

This is a one-candle-only forecast.

It expires immediately after the next 15m candle.
