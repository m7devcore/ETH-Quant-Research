# 28. V6 Changes Learned From V5

## 1. Failure of acceptance > appearance of rejection

Priority should move toward:

**Breakout attempt → failure → re-entry → follow-through evidence**

rather than:

**price near extreme → wick → short/long**

---

## 2. Major level validity and entry timing are separate

A structural ceiling may be correct for hours or days while repeated 15m fade entries are wrong.

Future analysis must have two separate questions:

### Structural question
Is this level still valid?

### Timing question
Has the market actually proved rejection strongly enough to enter against the current move?

Do not merge them.

---

## 3. Add attack-persistence state

Repeated approaches to a major high/low are information.

V6 should test:
- number of attacks,
- higher lows into resistance,
- lower highs into support,
- time spent near the boundary,
- whether pullbacks are becoming shallower.

This may help distinguish:
- genuine rejection,
- absorption before breakout.

This concept must be tested on new untouched data before becoming a rule.

---

## 4. 5m candle color is not sufficient confirmation

V4 already warned this.

V5 confirms it again.

Future 5m confirmation should require a structural event such as:
- micro swing break,
- reclaim/loss,
- retest failure,
- minimum retracement.

But no specific replacement is promoted yet because post-hoc samples were too small.

---

## 5. Keep shock filters, but do not confuse “no shock” with “no trend”

20–22 July showed orderly repricing rather than one giant shock.

Daily/4H body filters passed, yet local fades failed.
