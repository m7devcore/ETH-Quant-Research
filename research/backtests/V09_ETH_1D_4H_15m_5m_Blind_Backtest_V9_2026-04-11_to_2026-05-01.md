# 8. The V8 Fix — Range Midpoint Stability

V8 treated low ER as enough evidence for a range.

V9 adds a second question:

> Is the center of the local 15m value area itself stable?

Define:

- current 24-bar 15m range midpoint
- 24-bar range midpoint measured 6 candles earlier

Then:

**midpoint drift = |current midpoint − prior midpoint| / 15m ATR(7)**

If this drift is too large:

# NO FADE

This is the key structural correction added in V9.

---

# 9. V9 Study Family

The stable family became:

1. 4H ER(6) must be low/moderate.
2. Previous 3 completed 15m candles must create a large displacement.
3. The 24-bar local range midpoint must remain relatively stable.
4. Predict a one-candle mean reversion opposite that 3-bar displacement.

This avoids relying on:
- RSI alone,
- one wick,
- one 5m candle,
- static support/resistance.
