# 1. Important Validity Note

This report is **procedurally blind, but not perfectly epistemically blind**.

V3 previously used **1–14 August 2026** as its study period. Therefore prices from **8–14 August** already exist inside the historical training-source set.

That makes it impossible to truthfully claim that V4's validation week was completely unknown to the broader conversation.

To reduce contamination as much as possible:

- the V4 signal rule was calculated only from **25 July–7 August** data,
- the hidden-week result was not used to choose the official rule,
- model candidates that overfit the study period were rejected before forward scoring,
- all post-reveal diagnostics are explicitly separated from the official V4 result.

This caveat materially lowers the evidentiary strength of V4 compared with a genuinely untouched historical week.

# 2. Objective

The user's long-term objective is to reduce 15m directional mistakes as far as possible.

V4 therefore changes the hierarchy:

### 1D
Macro condition / shock state.

### 4H
Regime filter and structural environment.

### 15m
Primary setup and the candle whose next direction is forecast.

### 5m
Micro confirmation only.

The model is allowed to return:

# NO TRADE

rather than predict every 15m candle.

Zero error cannot be assumed or guaranteed. The test measures how far the methodology actually gets.

# 3. Lessons Imported From V1, V2 and V3

## V1
Main errors:
- fixed directional bias,
- overweighting lagging averages,
- treating resistance as a guaranteed rejection,
- underestimating breakout continuation.

Permanent lesson:

**Trigger → Confirmation → Invalidation → Flip → Reassessment**

## V2
Main errors:
- small-sample overfitting,
- incorrect confidence calibration,
- forcing 15m direction when there was no edge,
- failure to classify regime before choosing the rule.

Permanent additions:
- **NO TRADE is valid**
- confidence must improve out-of-sample results
- regime comes before direction.

## V3
Main improvements:
- 4H range → acceptance → regime flip worked much better,
- 5m was demoted from independent direction source to execution confirmation.

Main remaining errors:
- a regime change had no expiry,
- lower-timeframe rules were not reset fast enough after structural change,
- 15m range rules could fail when the regime changed,
- 5m apparent edges could disappear out of sample.

V4 therefore adds:
- Daily shock filter,
- 4H shock filter,
- 4H non-trend gate,
- selective 15m stretch setup,
- 5m reversal confirmation.

# 6. Official Frozen V4 Rule

The V4 rule was frozen before official forward scoring.

A forecast is produced only when **all** conditions are met.

## Layer 1 — Daily shock gate
Using the most recently completed daily candle:

**Daily candle body ≤ 0.9 × average range of the previous 3 daily candles**

If not:

**NO TRADE**

Purpose:
avoid using a range/reversal rule immediately after a macro daily shock.

---

## Layer 2 — 4H regime gate

The most recently completed 4H candle must satisfy:

### Efficiency Ratio
**4H ER(6) ≤ 0.34**

This means the recent 4H path is not efficiently trending.

### Shock filter
**4H candle body ≤ 0.8 × 4H ATR(7)**

If either condition fails:

**NO TRADE**

Purpose:
do not use a mean-reversion 15m setup during an established 4H trend or fresh 4H shock.

---

## Layer 3 — 15m stretch trigger

### Bearish fade setup
The previous three completed 15m candles must produce:

- 3-bar movement ≥ **+1.1 × 15m ATR(7)**
- RSI(7) ≥ **60**

Provisional forecast:

**next 15m = DOWN**

### Bullish fade setup
The previous three completed 15m candles must produce:

- 3-bar movement ≤ **-1.1 × 15m ATR(7)**
- RSI(7) ≤ **40**

Provisional forecast:

**next 15m = UP**

---

## Layer 4 — 5m reversal confirmation

For a bullish reversal:
- 5m RSI ≤ 45,
- last 5m candle bullish,
- close location > 55% of its range.

For a bearish reversal:
- 5m RSI ≥ 55,
- last 5m candle bearish,
- close location < 45% of its range.

Without 5m reversal confirmation:

**NO TRADE**

# 13. What V4 Got Right

## 13.1 It stopped predicting most candles
V2 showed that forcing every 15m candle toward UP or DOWN was effectively random.

V4 forecast only:

**24 of 672 candles = 3.57%**

This is a real improvement in discipline.

---

## 13.2 Daily shock protection worked after 10 August
10 August produced:

- high: **$1,937.67**
- low: **$1,866.14**
- close: **$1,878.76**
- daily move: **-2.15%**

After that shock, the Daily gate blocked the full 11 August signal set.

This followed the V3 lesson:

**after a shock, rebuild rather than continue the old lower-timeframe state.**

---

## 13.3 The model worked better in calm rotational conditions
9 August:
**4 / 5 = 80%**

14 August:
**3 / 4 = 75%**

These days more closely matched the environment for which the fade rule was designed:
low 4H efficiency, limited macro shock, and local 15m overextension.

---

## 13.4 The four-timeframe hierarchy was respected
The final signal required:

1. Daily not shocked,
2. 4H not trending / shocked,
3. 15m stretched,
4. 5m reversal confirmation.

The 5m chart did not independently choose direction.

That preserves one of V3's strongest lessons.

# 14. What V4 Got Wrong

## 14.1 The main objective was not achieved
The stated objective was to move toward zero 15m mistakes.

Actual result:

**10 mistakes from 24 signals.**

That is far from zero.

No wording or scoring should hide this.

---

## 14.2 10 August exposed the largest weakness
10 August:
- 4 signals,
- 1 correct,
- 3 wrong,
- accuracy: **25%**

The most damaging errors included:

### 01:00
Forecast:
**DOWN**

Actual:
**UP +0.464%**

MAE:
**0.643%**

The model interpreted an upward stretch as exhaustion, but the move was actually continuing toward the day's high.

### 15:45
Forecast:
**UP**

Actual:
**DOWN -0.500%**

MAE:
**0.566%**

The model interpreted a downside stretch as exhaustion while a real breakdown was still expanding.

These are mirror-image failures.

The common cause:

# V4 confused early regime transition with range exhaustion.

---

## 14.3 4H Efficiency Ratio was too slow
At the major 10 Aug errors, 4H ER remained very low:

- around **0.07**
- around **0.03**

That told V4 "range / inefficient movement."

But price was already transitioning violently.

Therefore:

**low ER does not guarantee safe mean reversion.**

A low-efficiency environment can become a directional break before ER reacts.

---

## 14.4 The Daily shock filter was necessarily late
The Daily filter correctly blocked 11 August.

But it could only know the 10 August daily shock **after the daily candle completed**.

It therefore cannot protect the first day of a new regime by itself.

This is not a coding bug.

It is a structural limitation of using completed Daily candles.

---

## 14.5 One 5m reversal candle was too weak
Several misses had a valid-looking single 5m reversal candle, but the next 15m still continued the original stretch.

Examples:
- 10 Aug 01:00
- 10 Aug 15:45
- 13 Aug 08:00
- 14 Aug 09:45

The 5m confirmation was often:

**a temporary pause, not a true reversal.**

---

## 14.6 Accuracy did not translate into good excursion quality
The MAE/MFE imbalance is a serious weakness.

Even some correct close-direction calls had significant adverse movement first.

This means "58.33% correct candles" is not enough evidence for an executable trading edge.

**extreme extension sometimes reverses, but the first reversal attempt during a structural transition is not automatically safe.**

# 16. Why the Errors Happened

The errors fall into three main classes.

## Class A — Breakout / breakdown continuation mistaken for exhaustion
Examples:
- 10 Aug 01:00
- 10 Aug 15:45
- 13 Aug 08:00

Problem:
the rule faded a move that was still expanding.

## Class B — 5m false reversal
Examples:
- 8 Aug 06:45
- 9 Aug 16:45
- 13 Aug 06:30

Problem:
one 5m reversal candle was insufficient confirmation.

## Class C — oversold / overbought remained extreme
Example:
- 14 Aug 09:45

15m RSI was approximately 20.6 and 5m RSI approximately 23.7, yet price still continued lower.

Problem:

# Oversold does not mean the next candle must rise.

# 17. Post-Reveal Diagnostics — NOT Part of Official V4

After revealing the week, stronger filters were tested only to understand the mistakes.

## 17.1 Structural-boundary buffer
Avoiding fades too close to a 4H range boundary produced only small improvement.

The hidden result remained around:

**56–59%** for useful sample sizes.

Therefore a simple "do not fade near the 4H boundary" rule is not a complete fix.

## 17.2 Two-candle 5m confirmation
Requiring two 5m reversal candles raised one hidden subset to:

**62.5%**

But it produced only **8 hidden signals**, and only **1 signal in the first internal study segment**.

That sample is too small to treat as a validated improvement.

## Conclusion
There is no honest post-hoc modification that turns V4 into a zero-error system.

# 18. V5 Lessons

V5 should test these ideas on a genuinely untouched period.

## 1. Transition detector before mean reversion
A dedicated "possible regime transition" state is needed.

Possible evidence:
- 15m expansion in range + volume,
- repeated closes near candle extreme,
- rapid 4H structural-level attack,
- absence of meaningful 5m retracement.

If transition risk is elevated:

**NO FADE**

---

## 2. Stronger 5m confirmation
One candle is too weak.

Future tests should evaluate:
- two-candle reversal,
- 5m swing break,
- reclaim/loss of a micro structural level,
- minimum retracement size.

But only if the rule survives a genuinely unseen period.

---

## 3. Structural acceptance and rejection must outrank RSI
If price is attacking a major structural level:

**structure > oscillator**

Do not fade solely because RSI is high or low.

---

## 4. Daily is a context layer, not an early warning system
A completed Daily candle is useful for:
- shock classification,
- broader range/trend context,
- post-shock reset.

It is too slow to identify the first intraday transition.

---

## 5. Zero-error objective should be pursued by abstention, not false certainty
The only legitimate path toward fewer mistakes is:

- fewer signals,
- stronger confirmation,
- more NO TRADE,
- untouched forward testing.

Not:
- fitting more indicators until history looks perfect.
