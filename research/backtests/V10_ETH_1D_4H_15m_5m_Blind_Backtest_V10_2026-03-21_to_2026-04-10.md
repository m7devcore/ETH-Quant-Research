# 1. Why V10 Exists

V7 proved that extreme selectivity can produce a perfect-looking result on a tiny sample.

V8 proved that simply loosening filters to generate more signals destroys precision.

V9 improved the trade-off:

- hidden signals: **24**
- hidden accuracy: **66.67%**
- coverage: **3.57%**

V10 therefore had two simultaneous objectives:

1. **increase the number of independently testable 15m predictions, ideally by roughly 2× or more,**
2. **avoid reintroducing rules already disproven in V1–V9.**

The result is scientifically useful even though the final hidden precision did not improve.

# 2. Source Review: V1 → V9

All prior reports were treated as source material before V10 development.

## V1
Keep:
- Trigger → Confirmation → Invalidation → Flip → Reassessment.
- accepted price action can invalidate the old regime.
- do not defend a forecast because of lagging averages.

## V2
Keep:
- regime before direction.
- NO TRADE is valid.
- in-sample edge is not enough.

Retire:
- forced prediction on every candle.

## V3
Keep:
- regime expiry/reset.
- 5m as execution / confirmation rather than primary directional authority.

## V4
Keep:
- early transition can destroy mean reversion.
- extreme RSI does not prove immediate reversal.

Retire:
- one 5m reversal candle as sufficient reversal proof.

## V5
Keep:
- failure of acceptance is more meaningful than visual rejection.
- repeated attacks / persistence matter.

Retire:
- Grade B outer-rejection + single-5m setup.
- treating a correct structural level as automatically correct entry timing.

## V6
Keep:
- re-test inherited rules on every new sample.
- borderline regime quality must not receive full confidence.

## V7
Keep:
- deeper displacement/reset can improve selectivity.
- one-candle horizon.

Do not universalize:
- exact V7 trend-pullback formula; later periods did not reproduce it reliably.

## V8
Permanent lesson:
- **low ER is not the same thing as a stable range.**
- RSI and range-edge exhaustion can fail badly during repricing.

Retire:
- low-ER + RSI/edge fade as a universal setup.

## V9
Keep:
- local-value / midpoint migration is useful context.
- signal clustering can mean the first reversal call is early.
- stronger multi-candle displacement deserves testing.

Do not universalize:
- fixed 1.20/1.30 ATR thresholds with hard ER gate.
- 45-minute cooldown as a universal repair.

# 6. Candidate Work Before Freeze

## 6.1 Exact V9 logic did not transfer cleanly

The V9 hard combination of:
- ER gate,
- 3-bar displacement threshold,
- midpoint-drift gate

lost much of its prior advantage in V10 study.

The V9 post-reveal **1.30 ATR** idea also failed to reproduce its V9 strength reliably.

Decision:

**do not inherit V9 parameters blindly.**

---

## 6.2 45-minute cooldown was not stable

A cooldown could raise selected in-sample accuracies while reducing signal count, but the internal holdout did not consistently improve.

Decision:

**cooldown is not a universal V10 rule.**

---

## 6.3 Acceleration-continuation branch failed

V10 explicitly tested the opposite thesis:

> if value is migrating and displacement is aligned, predict continuation instead of reversal.

Simple variants based on:
- midpoint drift,
- displacement,
- 4H slope,
- accepted breakout

were broadly poor, often only about **38–46%**.

Decision:

**do not add a weak continuation branch merely to expand coverage.**

# 7. Multi-Horizon Exhaustion Discovery

The strongest recurring study behavior was next-candle mean reversion after sufficiently large multi-candle displacement.

The important discovery was that the phenomenon existed on more than one horizon.

## 3-bar family

A 3-bar displacement around **1.8–1.9 ATR** produced roughly mid/high-70% study accuracy.

Example:

- ≥1.8 ATR:
  - 77 signals
  - 59 hits
  - **76.62%**
  - temporal segments: **80.00 / 75.86 / 72.22%**
  - internal holdout: **70.59%**

## 6-bar family

A 6-bar displacement around **2.0–2.2 ATR**, with a loose midpoint-migration cap, produced a second usable set of reversal candidates.

# 10. Official Frozen V10 Rule

The following was frozen **before 4 April was queried**.

## Branch A3 — short-horizon exhaustion

Previous 3 completed 15m candles:

**absolute displacement ≥ 1.90 × ATR(7)**

Then predict the next 15m candle in the **opposite** direction.

No mandatory:
- RSI,
- ER,
- EMA,
- 5m confirmation,
- midpoint gate.

---

## Branch B6 — longer-horizon exhaustion

Previous 6 completed 15m candles:

**absolute displacement ≥ 2.20 × ATR(7)**

AND the absolute shift of the 24-bar local midpoint over 6 candles must be:

**≤ 0.80 × ATR(7)**

Then predict the next 15m candle in the **opposite** direction.

---

## Union

- If A3 alone fires → use A3 reversal.
- If B6 alone fires → use B6 reversal.
- If both fire and agree → one signal.
- If both fire but disagree → **NO TRADE**.
- Every signal expires after **one 15m candle**.

# 25. What V10 Actually Taught

Real phenomenon.

False.
