## 5. Frozen Official V14 Rule

### Local range
- Use the prior **40 completed 15m candles**, excluding the signal candle.
- `RangeHigh`, `RangeLow`, `RangeMid`.
- Compute the equivalent 40-bar range ending **6 candles earlier**.
- Require absolute midpoint drift:
  `|CurrentMid - ShiftedMid| ≤ 1.00 × ATR(7)`.

### Failed breakout
**Upper failed breakout**
- signal high `≥ RangeHigh + 0.10 × ATR7`;
- signal close `≤ RangeHigh - 0.05 × ATR7`;
- raw prediction = next 15m **DOWN**.

**Lower failed breakout**
- signal low `≤ RangeLow - 0.10 × ATR7`;
- signal close `≥ RangeLow + 0.05 × ATR7`;
- raw prediction = next 15m **UP**.

### 4H alignment veto
Using the latest completed native Binance 4H candle:

Strong UPTREND:
- `ER(6) ≥ 0.40`
- `EMA5 > EMA13`
- EMA13 slope over 3 completed 4H bars > 0.

Strong DOWNTREND:
- `ER(6) ≥ 0.40`
- `EMA5 < EMA13`
- EMA13 slope over 3 completed 4H bars < 0.

If the **breakout direction** is aligned with that strong 4H trend, **NO TRADE** instead of fading.

### Expiry
- exactly one next 15m candle;
- no cooldown;
- no mandatory 5m confirmation;
- no D1 filter;
- all other states = `NO TRADE`.

## 17. POST-REVEAL Diagnostic: Boundary Pressure

The strongest new observation was not a new ATR threshold.

A post-reveal diagnostic counted whether the same range boundary had already been attacked during the previous **8 completed 15m candles**.

When keeping only signals with **zero prior same-edge attacks** in that short window:
- Hidden signals fell from 22 to **6**
- Hits = **4**
- Accuracy = **66.67%**

This is **not** a V14 rescue:
- it was discovered after Hidden was known;
- the sample is only six signals;
- it must be tested on a completely untouched block.

But the concept is more coherent than simply tightening a numeric threshold:

> **A first clean rejection may be different from the fourth or fifth rejection of the same boundary. Repeated tests can represent pressure/acceptance rather than independent reversal evidence.**

## 18. Other Post-Reveal Checks

None provided a robust fix:

- signed midpoint migration filters: generally ~33–44% Hidden;
- 15m ER/displacement vetoes: unstable and usually low coverage;
- 15m EMA trend alignment veto: removed almost everything;
- simple signal-candle body direction: no rescue;
- range-edge migration alone: best small variants around ~50%;
- counting outer-quartile closes improved some variants but did not create a stable edge.

So V14 should **not** be summarized as “we just need a better trend filter.”

## 19. Main Scientific Lesson

V12 taught:
- a local failed breakout can fail inside accepted higher-timeframe repricing.

V14 adds:
- **even when 4H has not yet recognized a strong trend, repeated local boundary pressure can already invalidate the fade.**

The missing state variable appears to be closer to:

**fresh rejection vs repeated boundary occupation/pressure**

rather than simply:
- trend vs range;
- high ER vs low ER;
- deep vs shallow wick;
- strong vs weak close-back.

A failed breakout is not necessarily an independent event. Context from the **previous attacks on the same edge** may matter materially.

## 20. Why the Learning Improved Even Though Accuracy Did Not

V14 did something useful despite failing:

1. It **falsified** the V13 deep-pullback simplification on untouched Study data.
2. It independently recovered the V12 higher-timeframe veto idea on a different regime.
3. It showed that even a broad Study parameter plateau can collapse OOS.
4. It exposed a new failure class: **early local transition before the 4H trend filter activates**.
5. It identified repeated boundary attacks as a cleaner hypothesis for the next test.

This is exactly the kind of learning we want: fewer assumptions survive each round.
