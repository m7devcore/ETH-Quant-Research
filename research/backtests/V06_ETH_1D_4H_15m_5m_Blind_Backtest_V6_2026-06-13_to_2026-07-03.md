# 10. Official Frozen V6 Rule

A directional prediction is made only when all stages pass.

## Stage 1 — 4H Trend State

Calculate:

- 4H ER(6)
- EMA5
- EMA13
- EMA13 slope over 3 completed 4H candles

### Bullish trend
- ER(6) ≥ **0.34**
- EMA5 > EMA13
- EMA13 3-bar slope > 0

### Bearish trend
- ER(6) ≥ **0.34**
- EMA5 < EMA13
- EMA13 3-bar slope < 0

Otherwise:

# TRANSITION → NO TRADE

---

## Stage 2 — 15m Countertrend Pullback

Using completed 15m candles:

### In a bullish 4H trend
The previous 3 completed 15m candles must move down by at least:

**1.00 × 15m ATR(7)**

### In a bearish 4H trend
The previous 3 completed 15m candles must move up by at least:

**1.00 × 15m ATR(7)**

This prevents entering trend continuation after an already extended trend-direction move.

---

## Stage 3 — 5m Resume Confirmation

The latest two completed 5m candles must move back in the 4H trend direction.

The net two-candle move must be at least:

**0.15 × 5m ATR(7)**

Only then:

### Bull trend
Predict next 15m:
**UP**

### Bear trend
Predict next 15m:
**DOWN**

Otherwise:

# NO TRADE
