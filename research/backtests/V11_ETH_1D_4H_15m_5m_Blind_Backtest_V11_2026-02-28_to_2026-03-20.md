**absolute close-to-close displacement ≥ 1.60 × 15m ATR(7)**

## Layer 2 — 15m longer-horizon efficiency

Calculate **ER(12)** on completed 15m candles.

Require:

**ER(12) ≤ 0.20**

Interpretation:

The recent 6-bar move is large, but the broader 12-bar path is still inefficient enough to resemble local exhaustion rather than clean directional repricing.

**NO TRADE**

## Layer 3 — 5m timing confirmation

Use the most recently completed 5m candle before the target 15m candle.

If the 6-bar displacement was upward:

- latest completed 5m candle must be bearish;
- predict next 15m **DOWN**.

If the 6-bar displacement was downward:

- latest completed 5m candle must be bullish;
- predict next 15m **UP**.

No minimum 5m body-size threshold.

No second 5m candle requirement.

## Layer 4 — Expiry

Prediction horizon:

# NEXT 15m ONLY

The signal expires immediately after that candle.

## No mandatory additions

The frozen rule did **not** include:

- 4H ER gate;

# 23. Main V11 Scientific Lesson

The central V11 hypothesis was:

> strong 6-bar displacement + low 12-bar efficiency + one 5m reversal candle can identify true exhaustion.

Study strongly supported it.

Hidden did not.

Therefore the correct lesson is not to find one more threshold.

The correct lesson is:

# Low longer-horizon efficiency is still not enough to prove that a strong local move has completed its price discovery.

In other words:

- V8 taught **low 4H ER ≠ stable range**.
- V11 now adds:

# low 15m ER12 ≠ guaranteed local exhaustion.

A market can:

- rotate over 3 hours,
- then make a strong 90-minute displacement,
- print a 5m counter-candle,
- and still continue or delay the reversal by one or more 15m candles.

The 5m confirmation improved Study timing but did not solve Hidden timing.
