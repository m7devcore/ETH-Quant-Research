# 4. V18 Pre-Registered Research Features

V18 kept the V17 continuation structure but added two research dimensions:

1. **Impulse path efficiency**
2. **4H maturity / extension risk**

The following normalized variables were used.

## 4.1 Impulse Magnitude

For signal candle `i`:

`Impulse = Close[i-4] - Close[i-10]`

Direction:

`D = sign(Impulse)`

Normalized magnitude:

`I = |Impulse| / ATR7_15m`

---

## 4.2 Impulse Efficiency

`E_I = ER6` measured at the end of the impulse window.

This distinguishes:
- a directional impulse,
from:
- a large close-to-close displacement produced by noisy alternating movement.

---

## 4.3 Pullback Magnitude

`Pullback = Close[i] - Close[i-4]`

Require direction opposite `D`.

Normalized pullback:

`P = |Pullback| / ATR7_15m`

---

## 4.4 Relative Reset Ratio

`R = |Pullback| / |Impulse|`

This measures how much of the original impulse was retraced.

---

## 4.5 Pullback Efficiency

`E_P = ER4`

This measures whether the four-bar reset was directional or choppy.

# 5. Continuous Quality Equations Tested

Because the user explicitly requested algorithmic equations rather than only discrete thresholds, V18 tested several continuous forms.

These equations were Study-only candidates.

## Q1 — Balanced Structural Quality

`Q1 = I × E_I × E_P / (1 + R)`

Purpose:
reward directional impulse and directional reset while penalizing excessive retracement.

## Q2 — Efficiency With Overrun Penalty

Define:

`O = max(0, R - 1)`

Then:

`Q2 = sqrt(E_I × E_P) × I / (1 + O)`

Purpose:
do not punish normal reset too heavily, but penalize retracement beyond the full impulse.

## Q3 — Explicit Full-Retrace Penalty

`Q3 = I × E_I × E_P × max(0.15, 1 - O)`

## Q4 — Relative Structural Advantage

`Q4 = [I / (R + 0.35)] × sqrt(E_I × E_P)`

### Result

None of the continuous-score thresholds produced a broader and more stable Study edge than the simpler interpretable filters.

The best composite-score families were around **70% Study**, similar to a simple impulse-efficiency filter.

Therefore:

> V18 did not promote a magic combined score.

The equations remain useful diagnostics for V19, but not validated trade gates.
