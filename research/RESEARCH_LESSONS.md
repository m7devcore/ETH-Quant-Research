# V1–V22 lessons — navigation summary, not a replacement for originals

- **V01**: Never defend an invalidated fixed price thesis; trigger → confirmation → invalidation → flip → reassess.
- **V02**: Classify regime before direction; `NO TRADE` is a legitimate outcome.
- **V03**: A regime needs expiry/reset; 5m should be execution/confirmation, not a stand-alone directional engine.
- **V04**: Early shock/transition invalidates naive mean reversion; oscillator extremes and a lone 5m counter-candle are insufficient.
- **V05**: Failed acceptance is stronger evidence than a rejection-looking wick.
- **V06**: Marginal/borderline 4H trend strength is not the same as a strong trend.
- **V07**: Very high precision on a tiny number of trades is statistically weak.
- **V08**: Low Efficiency Ratio (ER) does NOT guarantee a stable range.
- **V09**: Local value/midpoint migration matters; test parameter neighborhoods, not one lucky threshold.
- **V10**: High displacement can be repricing rather than reversal exhaustion; increasing coverage may destroy edge.
- **V11**: 87.50% Study fell to 54.55% Hidden; local pauses may occur inside accepted trend expansion.
- **V12**: 66.04% Study fell to 50.00% Hidden; a local failed breakout does not guarantee higher-timeframe rejection.
- **V13**: 80.00% Study fell to 36.36% Hidden; identifying trend does not solve entry timing.
- **V14**: 75.00% Study fell to 36.36% Hidden; repeated boundary occupation changes meaning of rejection.
- **V15**: 66.67% Study fell to 44.68% Hidden; stronger impulse alone does not guarantee continuation.
- **V16**: Relative retracement geometry improves description but 82.14% Study fell to 58.33% Hidden.
- **V17**: Clean reset/pullback efficiency was useful in a small Hidden sample; don't overgeneralize from 9 signals.
- **V18**: Regime permission + setup quality + structural survival; a small-sample 80% is not proof.
- **V19**: Formal separation of regime permission, setup quality, structural survival, and prediction/abstention.
- **V20**: Volatility/tradeability filter alone did not transport; higher local volatility damaged old directional rules.
- **V21**: Single-regime walk-forward and balanced classifiers collapsed; Hidden 45.45%. Internal validation is not sufficient for regime transport.
- **V22**: Three logistic experts on 18 historic Study regimes (A=V16–V21, B=V10–V15, C=V4–V9), 18 features, probability average with agreement and support gates. Historical 1619/2733=59.24%, Adaptation 95/159=59.75%, Fresh 48/81=59.26%. Wilson interval includes 50%; original checkpoint is missing and fees/slippage have not been tested.

**Research discipline**: historical reported accuracy may be correct for a particular split but cannot be generalized without independent reproducible data and leakage checks. The original V01..V22 reports are the authoritative documents for their own results. Do not summarize old failures as permanent signal rules.
